About

Why C.U.D. Exists

C.U.D. was created from a simple observation:
I travel more than ever.
Yet sometimes I feel less connected than ever.
To myself.
To other people.
To the places I visit.
C.U.D. was created to make space for something different.

More than travel. It's a way of living.

C.U.D. is not about escaping your life.
It's about meaningful experiences and conversations,
slow mornings and deep connections.
I don't collect places.
I collect people and moments that
truly stay with you.

Who is behind C.U.D.

I’m Piotr.

I’m the founder and curator of C.U.D. Experience (short for “Curated Human Experiences”), my small, independent slow-travel brand. I design and personally host small-group experiences in Asia, built around real conversations, local culture and unhurried days.

I personally curate every C.U.D. experience — from the place you wake up in to the table you sit around — and I read every application myself. I run C.U.D. as an individual based in Poland, and this website is informational: applications are not bookings.

Piotr Paweł Kamiński — Founder & Curator, C.U.D. Experience

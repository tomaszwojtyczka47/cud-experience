Application

Apply to Join C.U.D. Origins™

Hoi An, Vietnam · 20–30 March 2027 · 11 days · 10 nights · only 9 participants

C.U.D. is intentionally small. Not everyone who applies is invited to join.

I want to create a small group of people who are curious, open, considerate of others, and ready to experience Hoi An — and the people around them — in a more intentional way. I'm not looking for one specific personality type. You don't need to be an extrovert. You don't need to love group trips. You don't need to be the most talkative person at the table. What matters most is whether your way of travelling fits this particular experience.

Take a few minutes and answer honestly. There are no "right" answers here.

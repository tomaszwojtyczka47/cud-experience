01 — The Idea

10 — This Is / Isn’t for You

For those who seek more than just a trip.

This experience is for you if…

  • You prefer meaningful experiences over busy itineraries.
  • You enjoy meeting people who don’t share your background.
  • You can spend an afternoon without needing to be entertained.
  • You are curious about local life.
  • You value good food, beautiful spaces and honest conversations.
  • You want to travel differently.

This experience is NOT for you if…

  • You are looking for a party holiday.
  • You want a packed itinerary from morning until night.
  • You prefer travelling anonymously in large groups.
  • You expect every moment to be planned for you.
  • You are uncomfortable with different personalities, cultures or perspectives.

14 — FAQ

Good questions to ask.

  • Can I come alone?

    Absolutely. In fact, many C.U.D. guests may arrive without knowing anyone. You won’t be travelling alone for long.

  • How do I get to Hoi An?

    The closest airport is Da Nang International Airport (DAD), about 45 minutes from Hoi An. Full arrival details are shared after your application is accepted.

  • Is the experience suitable for solo travellers?

    Yes. Most C.U.D. guests travel solo — five of the seven private spaces are designed for one person.

  • Can I come with a partner or friend?

    Yes. Two private cottages are designed specifically for two people travelling together.

  • What happens after I apply?

    Applications are reviewed personally by Piotr. If it feels like a good fit, you’ll hear back with next steps and the full itinerary.

  • How many people will be there?

    Nine guests, plus Piotr. C.U.D. Origins™ is intentionally small.

  • What is included?

    Accommodation, most meals, wellness sessions, transfers and all curated experiences — see What’s Included above for the full list.

  • What isn’t included?

    International flights, travel insurance, alcohol and any personal spending are not included.

  • How does payment work?

    A 30% deposit secures your place. The remaining balance is payable in agreed instalments before departure.

  • Can I choose my room?

    Rather than selecting from a list, tell us how you’re travelling and we’ll help you find the space that fits you best.

  • Do I need to be physically fit?

    No special fitness level is required. The pace is slow, and every activity can be adapted to what feels right for you.

  • Is alcohol included?

    Not as standard, but it’s available locally and can be part of shared meals where appropriate.

  • What happens if I need to cancel?

    Cancellation terms are shared with your application details — reach out any time if your plans change.

  • When do I receive the full itinerary?

    The full day-by-day itinerary is shared after your application is accepted.
